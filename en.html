Our approach

Preparing for the AI‑native industry

EPAM School of Digital Technologies opened in 2022 at American University Kyiv. We prepare engineers, designers and researchers for the digital product industry, where artificial intelligence has become an everyday working tool.

Our programs combine fundamental knowledge, an AI-first approach and real engineering experience: students learn to American standards together with Arizona State University, and EPAM experts bring industry practice into the classroom.

A strong foundation

AI amplifies knowledge, it does not replace it. That is why we build a solid foundation in programming, algorithms, mathematics and computer science.

AI-First Engineering

Students use AI for analysis, design, programming, testing and research, exactly as it is done in the IT industry today.

Projects and internships

Real projects, industry collaboration and internships help students build a portfolio while still studying.
